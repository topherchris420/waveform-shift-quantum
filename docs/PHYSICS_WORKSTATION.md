# Physics workstation boundary

The repository's quantum and ARFR workstations remain independent of the Systemic Stress & Coordination Lab.

## Quantum workstation (`/`)

The interface compares established quantum calculations with the proposed Woodyard field-modulated localization model. Established equations, proposed extensions, interpretive claims, testable predictions, and falsification conditions retain their own labels. See the root README for the current formulas and scope.

The model comparison also states how each proposed number was produced: a derived prediction, a toy assumption, an illustrative transformation, or a speculative scenario. Non-derived values are drawn differently and labelled "not a model prediction". Model descriptors, unit declarations and observable definitions live in `src/lib/researchModels.ts`, `src/lib/units.ts` and `src/lib/observables.ts`. Research notes and the promotion path from proposal to protocol are in [research/gpd](../research/gpd/README.md).

## ARFR workstation (`/arfr`)

ARFR is a proposed physical-technology simulation with its own deterministic stepping, diagnostics, and passports. Its integrity and timing contract is documented in [ARFR_EXPERIMENT_INTEGRITY.md](ARFR_EXPERIMENT_INTEGRITY.md).

## Prohibited inference across workstations

Financial-network behavior does not validate a physical theory. Quantum or ARFR behavior does not validate an economic policy or coordination mechanism. Reusing TypeScript, visualization components, hashing primitives, seeds, or the word “Genesis” supplies software infrastructure only; it transfers no empirical evidence, causal warrant, or institutional endorsement.
